## Purpose

## Design

Use only tracked source/canonical docs, the accepted Block 2 provider-free package, and committed S13–S15 Outcomes. Generated S15 identities and raw provider content remain forbidden. Treat graph/delegate results as navigation until source-confirmed.

The source audit must distinguish:

- **trigger:** why a first provider response fails strict/deterministic validation;
- **cost amplifier:** the complete-projection repair call and provider reasoning/latency;
- **result semantics:** policy/status/finding differences after a valid response;
- **evidence durability:** whether started/completed/failed/uncertain calls and validation diagnostics survive interruption.

Current evidence proves the repair cost amplifier and historical durability defect, but not the trigger. It also proves no eligible human-reviewed case exists: the default calibration bundle is empty, S15's tracked case service explicitly attests non-human review/no human claim, and test fixtures/agent metadata cannot become human labels.
