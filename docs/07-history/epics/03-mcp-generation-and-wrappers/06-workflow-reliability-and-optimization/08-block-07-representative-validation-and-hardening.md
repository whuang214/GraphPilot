# Block 7: Representative Validation and Hardening

## Goal

Determine whether the optimized Block 6 workflow generalizes beyond its anchor/guard cases, remains stable over repeated
runs, and handles representative quality, failure, and recovery boundaries safely.

## Question This Block Owns

Does the optimized candidate work consistently across the diagram types, complexity, readiness boundaries, repairs, and
operational conditions GraphPilot must support, without overfitting or hidden regressions?

## Representative Coverage

Design the smallest reviewed matrix supported by Block 5–6 evidence. It may cover:

- Activity, Use Case, and BDD;
- basic and complex requests;
- ready, ready-with-warnings, and non-overrideable blocked boundaries;
- naturally reached readiness, generation, review-response, and candidate repair paths;
- cold/warm artifact/process/cache state;
- provider failure, client interruption, finalized recovery, and no-rerun behavior;
- repeated cells sufficient to evaluate boundary, finding, quality, repair, and performance stability.

Do not preselect a large matrix in this high-level roadmap. Human-reviewed labels/oracles, exact cases/repeats/order, hard
quality gates, call ceilings, stop rules, and resume policy require an audited block-specific package and explicit live
approval.

## Validation Dimensions

- Readiness status, score/facet bands, blockers, refs, actions, and false-ready/false-blocking.
- Required generated concepts/relationships/structured fields and unsupported additions.
- Canonical/schema/provenance/context-stability validity.
- Semantic-review status, findings, repair outcome, and final-review stability.
- Layout/persistence/render/editor success and parity.
- Host/Azure/MCP/local/browser timing, calls, tokens, credits, cache, and repairs.
- Outcome stability and worst cases per type/status/complexity cell.
- Timeout/recovery and uncertain-state terminal behavior.

No aggregate average hides a hard failing cell.

## Feedback to Optimization

A representative failure is attributed to its narrowest supported owner. Return material latency, reliability, or quality
issues to Block 6 as a measured optimization/hardening wave. Rerun the anchor, affected representative cells, and the complete
applicable matrix before declaring resolution. Never replace or favorably rerun a stopped/completed identity.

## Outputs

- Frozen representative package and immutable observations.
- Per-cell and aggregate quality/performance/stability report.
- Hard failure and uncertainty inventory.
- Block 6 feedback packages and rerun evidence when needed.
- Promotion-readiness recommendation and remaining certification prerequisites.

## Exit Gate

Every required representative cell passes its hard quality/reliability gates, repeated boundaries are sufficiently stable,
performance remains materially improved from the pre-optimization baseline, failure/recovery behavior is safe, unresolved
unknowns are explicit, and no stopped/failed identity was rerun or averaged away.

## Not Owned Here

- Introducing unmeasured optimization changes without returning to Block 6.
- Weakening labels/gates to pass the candidate.
- Replacing formal certification or automatically promoting defaults.
- Expanding cases/repeats after provider use without a new audited package and approval.

## Block-Agent Handoff

The validation agent returns exact package identities, schedule/budget actuals, case/cell results, failures, stability,
before/after performance, Block 6 feedback, checks/commits, and an explicit proceed/return/stop recommendation for high-level
review.
